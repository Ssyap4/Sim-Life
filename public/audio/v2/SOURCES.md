# Audio bank v2 / Sim-Life 0.47

All imported recordings below are CC0 1.0 at their source pages. The local assets are trimmed, normalized and converted to mono 24 kHz PCM WAV for Safari/Android decode compatibility. Sources are retained for audit. No runtime external audio requests. No Sims audio, commercial game soundtracks or original UFC/Forza recordings. Voices are licensed anonymous fictional speech clips, not recordings of the real players.
