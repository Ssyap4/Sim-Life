## Checkpoint 6 - bounded network transport and graceful restart
- Serialize broadcast packets once for all active peers. A peer with over 2 MiB pending outbound bytes is disconnected rather than accumulating ordered snapshots indefinitely; a healthy partner receives the same authoritative packet. Send exceptions are isolated.
- Client send now reports failure when disconnected, overloaded (512 KiB pending), or send throws. Recovery opens a fresh socket without replaying commands. Null/malformed outer packets and duplicate/wrong-role ready packets are ignored. Unauthenticated sockets have a 15-second deadline. Limits are engineering defaults, not measured bandwidth targets.
- Graceful close stops new upgrades and message processing, checkpoints elapsed whole game minutes, clears waiting queues and terminates sockets before disposing session registries. Concurrent close calls reuse one promise. SIGINT/SIGTERM call this close path. In-flight incomplete tasks are cancelled by normal disconnect handling; no general exactly-once protocol or hard-crash guarantee claimed.
